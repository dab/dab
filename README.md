### AI-assisted life, from the developer's seat

I'm Dima Belitsky. Full-stack engineer, UI first, ten-plus years in React, TypeScript and Node, currently pointed at one question: how does AI fit into the tooling around people without getting in the way? Dev workflow, personal advisor, home, quality of life. The model is the easy part. The seams are the work.
